Arrival & access

Getting in

Check-in

Check-in is from 15:00.

Arriving later? Please follow the access instructions we sent you before arrival.

Gate & access

  • During working hours: press 1 or 2 on the gate keypad.
  • After hours: use the gate PIN provided.
  • After 20:00: enter the Guesthouse with the door code provided.
  • Please make sure the main gate closes behind you each time you enter or leave.

Any difficulty getting in? Contact us on the numbers under Safety & contacts.

Parking

Secure parking is available on the property. Please park only in the designated guest areas.

Parking is at your own risk. Please don’t leave valuables in your vehicle.
During your stay

The essentials

Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is available throughout the guesthouse. The network name and password are displayed in your room.

We’re high in the valley, so we use a mobile internet connection rather than fibre. It’s usually stable but can be affected by weather conditions.

Morning coffee & light breakfast

Coffee and tea are available at any time on the bar counter.

A light continental breakfast is set out for self-service from 08:00 to 10:30 each morning.

Honesty bar

You’re welcome to enjoy the honesty bar. Please record your selections so they can be added to your account, or pay directly by scanning the QR code at the bar.

A guest home

Mont Bleu is designed as a guest home, not a formal guesthouse.

We are not a self-catering establishment, and we encourage you to visit the many world-class restaurants in town and at the wine estates in the valley. Our staff can help if something needs warming up in the microwave.

Shared spaces

Facilities

Pool, sauna & hot tub

Swimming pool

The pool is available for guest use. Please use the swimming towels provided in your room.

No lifeguard on duty. Please supervise children at all times and swim with care.

Sauna & hot tub

Our natural wood sauna and gas-fired hot tub offer a restorative mountain experience.

They’re available on request. Please speak to housekeeping to book a session, so they can prepare and heat it in advance. You can also book online.

Book the sauna or hot tub

Please rinse your feet before using the sauna or hot tub.

Television

Rooms have smart TVs for streaming. Please keep the volume low out of consideration for other guests.

Remember to sign out of your personal accounts before you leave.

Outdoor experiences

The farm is yours to explore

Mont Bleu gives you access to the natural surroundings of Le Sanctuaire Farm: a unique landscape of fynbos, forests, granite outcrops and streams.

Walking trails & waterfall

You’re welcome to explore the marked walking paths, the stream and the waterfall. See the farm map for routes.

A short river walk takes you to the natural waterfall pool, a beautiful spot for rest and reflection. The waterfall and all the stream pools are safe for swimming.

Wear suitable footwear and take extra care after rain.

Outdoor Gym

Please enjoy our Outdoor Gym near the Fruit Orchard — a great place to work off some of the calories gained from our delicious Franschhoek restaurants.

The Pilgrimage Path

The Le Sanctuaire pilgrimage walk invites quiet reflection through the landscape. Follow it at your own pace.

Housekeeping & guest services

Looking after your room

Housekeeping

Rooms are serviced daily. Use the Do Not Disturb and Make Up Room signs provided.

To help care for this valley’s precious water, towels are refreshed every three days unless you ask otherwise.

Laundry service

Laundry service is available. Complete the laundry form and place it inside the laundry bag for collection.

Nature awareness

A working natural landscape

Never feed the baboons. Wildlife may be present, especially our local baboons. Please observe them respectfully from a distance.
  • Stay on the marked paths.
  • Don’t approach animals.
  • Take care near water and on steep terrain.
Need assistance?

Just ask

If anything during your stay isn’t as you expected, or if you need help with anything at all, please let us know. We value the opportunity to help and to make your stay as comfortable as possible.

A final word

Go gently

We hope your stay at Mont Bleu brings rest, stillness and a deep sense of peace within this sanctuary of mountain, water and sky.

May you leave refreshed, restored by nature, and welcomed as part of the home.
